# Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Cinematic editorial documentary style for a serious history/current-affairs YouTube channel. Dark museum-like canvas, archival paper textures, restrained red accents, map lines, split-screen historical comparison, quiet institutional typography. The mood should feel intelligent, sober, and tense rather than flashy.

## Colors

- Background: `#0d1117`
- Ink: `#f2ead8`
- Muted text: `#a9b0bb`
- Archival paper: `#d8c7a2`
- China red accent: `#b7332f`
- Cold blue accent: `#4f7cac`

## Typography

- Headings: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif
- Body and captions: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif
- Monospace labels: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo

## What NOT To Do

- No neon cyberpunk palette.
- No busy collage that makes captions hard to read.
- No triumphant nationalist symbolism.
- No fake real-person photos.
- No decorative gradient orbs.

# Nixon/Trump Structural Reversal

## Purpose

This is the first sample project for the video production workflow.

It should replace the earlier abstract HTML mood-board sample with a watchable 60-90 second documentary-style opening draft.

## Core Thesis

Trump's 2026 China visit should not be treated as Nixon 2.0. The surface photograph rhymes with 1972, but the strategic structure is almost reversed: Nixon had the Soviet triangle pulling Washington and Beijing together; Trump does not have an equivalent shared third-power pressure.

# Image2 Prompts

These prompts are for Codex image generation only. They are not evidence. Do not use them as news footage, archive photography, or source material.

All images:

- 16:9.
- No real politicians.
- No readable fake documents.
- No logos.
- No fake screenshots.
- No news-photo style.
- Use as atmosphere, metaphor, or chapter background only.

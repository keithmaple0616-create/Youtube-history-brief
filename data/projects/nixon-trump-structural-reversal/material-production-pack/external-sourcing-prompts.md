# External Sourcing Prompts

Use these prompts to collect footage, archive photos, documents, maps, and source screenshots. Prefer official/public-domain/licensed sources. Record source URL, owner, license, and access date in `asset-intake-template.json`.

Do not scrape random news videos. Do not use unlicensed television footage as final material.

## Priority A: Opening Current Event / Diplomatic Visual Base

### src-001 Trump / U.S.-China diplomatic arrival footage

Suggested save path:

`assets/source/src-001-current-arrival-footage.mp4`

```text

C-SPAN U.S. president China visit arrival footage
Reuters/AP alternative only if licensed through creator account: Trump China visit arrival Beijing footage
```

```text

```

Usage:

Opening 0-8s. This is the real-world hook. If no exact 2026 footage is available, use neutral diplomatic arrival footage and avoid claiming it is the exact event.

Rights note:

Evidence/source label required if exact current-event footage is used.

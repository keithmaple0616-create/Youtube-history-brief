## 60-90 Second Opening Sample

Correct opening sequence:

```text
0-8s: real/current diplomatic arrival footage or neutral Beijing airport/diplomatic footage
8-16s: Nixon 1972 arrival archival footage/photo
16-24s: split-screen current-vs-1972 comparison
24-36s: press cameras / handshake / flags / table-setting source material
36-52s: simple triangle diagram: U.S. - China - Soviet Union
52-70s: return to current U.S.-China footage, no Soviet triangle
70-90s: title promise: not Nixon 2.0, but structural reversal
```

Minimum bar:

- At least 4 source-like shots.
- Only 1 diagram sequence.
- No AI politicians.
- No more than 1 full-screen title card.

## Section-Level Asset Counts

Minimum external assets before rendering:

- Current U.S.-China / Trump-China / diplomacy footage or source-like footage: 5-8 clips.
- Nixon 1972 China archive photos/footage: 6-10 items.
- Cold War / Sino-Soviet / Vietnam / Moscow context assets: 5-8 items.
- Economic relationship footage/documents: 6-10 items.
- Summit choreography / room / table / handshake assets: 4-6 items.
- Maps/documents/source screenshots: 8-12 items.

AI generated images:

- 5-7 maximum for the full video.
- Mostly chapter or metaphor backgrounds.
- Never evidence.

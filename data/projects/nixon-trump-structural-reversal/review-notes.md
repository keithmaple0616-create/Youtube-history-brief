# Review Notes

## What We Learned From The First Sample

The existing HTML sample is too abstract. It is useful as a mood board, but it feels like a presentation because most scenes are title cards, placeholder image boxes, and diagrams.

The next version should be judged by a different standard: does it feel like a documentary video within the first 10 seconds?

## Required Improvements

- Use the voiceover as the timing backbone from the start.
- Replace generic photo boxes with real or credible source-like visuals.
- Use quick editorial cuts in the opening, then slower archival pushes.
- Keep HyperFrames for the map/triangle explainer, not the whole video.
- Add basic music and sound design.
- Export a real MP4, not only a browser preview.

## Fact Checks Needed

- Verify whether the May 2026 Trump Beijing visit occurred as described.
- Verify official U.S. and Chinese statements about the visit.
- Verify which Nixon 1972 images are usable and how they must be credited.
- Verify the wording around "no equivalent Soviet shadow" so it does not imply Russia is irrelevant today.

## Placeholder Policy

Placeholders are allowed in early drafts, but they must be visibly tracked in `assets.json`.

Do not present AI-generated images of real historical or political figures as real footage or photography.

## Draft Acceptance Criteria

The next sample is acceptable if:

- It includes temporary AI voiceover.
- It includes at least 5 real or source-like visual beats.
- It includes one strong map/triangle explainer.
- It has music or light sound design.
- It exports as MP4.
- A non-technical viewer no longer describes it as a PPT.
